Sample data provenance
=======================

These images are a small subset (8 subjects, 7 images each = 56 images)
of CASIA-IrisV1, a public iris image database released by the Chinese
Academy of Sciences' Institute of Automation (CASIA) for research and
educational use: http://biometrics.idealtest.org/

They were sourced via a public GitHub mirror used in an educational
course project (github.com/ntachukwu/python-iris-recognition, MIT
licensed for the code; images are CASIA's, not that repo author's).

Included here purely for local development/testing of this school
prototype's biometric matching pipeline — not for redistribution or
any commercial use. If this project moves beyond a demo, replace this
with your own enrolled users' data, collected with proper consent and
under CASIA's own terms if their data is used again.
